Field Guide
01 Image Capture

The single biggest factor in a fast, clean run is flying high enough — coverage and ground resolution trade off, and doubling altitude roughly quarters the photo count. 200–330 ft AGL is the working band for most overlays. Drop to 100–160 ft only when fine detail truly matters.

Overlap matters more than total image count. Aim for 75% frontal and 65% side overlap. Most mission-planning apps calculate this — trust their planner over a manual count.

Fly an S-pattern grid over the target at consistent altitude — parallel passes across the area, reversing direction at each end. For high-detail jobs, fly the grid twice with the second pass rotated 90°. GPS lock must be solid before takeoff — take a single image on the ground to establish a baseline.

Regulatory note: Always comply with applicable FAA altitude regulations. In uncontrolled airspace the standard sUAS ceiling is 400 ft AGL; operations near controlled airspace or structures may require authorization. Confirm airspace status before flight.
Limit: 150 photos per job by default. Enable high-capacity mode below the photo picker to allow up to 300 photos per job.
02 Upload & Process

Enter the location of the incident in Section 1 — this becomes the output filename and how the overlay appears in your TAK client. Use a description your team will recognize (e.g. Eagle River Rd MP 12). A date-time stamp is appended automatically.

Select your GPS-tagged JPEG photos. The picker rejects non-JPEG files and photos missing GPS EXIF before processing starts.

Click Process. A 65-photo job at defaults takes about 3 minutes end-to-end. The page can be left open or closed — status updates on its own.

Optional toggles:

  • High-Resolution mode — pins output to 2.5 cm/px GSD (visibly finer detail). Runtime ~10 min for a 65-photo job.
  • Terrain correction — runs full 3D reconstruction; corrects geometric error from elevation change and tall vertical features (buildings, towers, rubble piles). Runtime ~35 min. Both modes together: ~42 min.
  • Save WebODM Task — retains the source WebODM project for inspection. Cleared at the 72-hour purge along with the job record.
03 Import to TAK

When a job completes, a GeoTIFF download button appears in the Downloads section above. Verified compatible with CloudTAK and TAKAware.

CloudTAK: open Overlay Tool > "+" > "Your Files", then use the "Upload Icon" to add the GeoTIFF.

Not to scale — unless the sUAS used RTK positioning, this overlay is not geometrically accurate and must not be used for measurements, boundary determinations, or any purpose requiring positional precision. Based on observed results, expect typical positional error of ±5 ft with extremes up to ±25 ft. Use for situational awareness only.
72-hour purge. The GeoTIFF is automatically deleted from the server 72 hours after the job completes.
